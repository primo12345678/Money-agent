# First revenue experiment

## Product: Lead Response Mini-System
For small service businesses that receive enquiries through a form or shared mailbox and lose track of follow-up.

### Deliverable
- One enquiry source connected to a client-owned Google Sheet.
- Acknowledgement to the enquirer using customer-approved wording and lawful basis.
- Notification to the owner/team.
- Follow-up reminder, lead status, handover notes and short operating guide.

### Test prices (not validated market rates)
- Paid pilot: €149.
- Standard setup: €299.
- Optional support: €39/month.

### Acquisition
1. Identify explicit public buyer requests.
2. Manually verify the post and whether the buyer still needs help.
3. Send a personal message only after owner approval and where platform rules permit.
4. Agree written scope, price and payment milestone.
5. Deliver in a client-owned account where practical.
6. Ask permission for an anonymized case study.

### Go/no-go
Review 20 qualified prospects, send 5 permitted personalized contacts, seek 2 meaningful replies and 1 paid pilot. These are experiment thresholds, not promises. If results fail, change the offer or channel before spending money.

Revenue means funds actually received, less refunds and direct costs. Never count lead scores or proposal values as revenue.
