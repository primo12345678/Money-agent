# First commercial offer: Lead Response Mini-System

## Decision

Money Agent will initially sell one narrow service: a lightweight enquiry capture and follow-up workflow for small service businesses.

This is a service business, not a SaaS product. We will sell a fixed-scope implementation pilot, deliver it manually with tested tools, and offer maintenance only after the customer confirms the workflow is useful.

## Customer problem

Small service businesses can lose work when enquiries arrive through a website form or shared mailbox and are not logged, acknowledged, assigned, or followed up consistently.

## Pilot deliverable

**Lead Response Mini-System — one intake channel, one tracker, one follow-up path.**

Included:
1. Map one agreed enquiry source (one form OR one mailbox).
2. Capture the minimum necessary fields in a customer-owned Google Sheet.
3. Create a clear pipeline: New, Replied, Follow-up due, Qualified, Won, Lost.
4. Notify the named business owner when a new enquiry arrives.
5. Configure an acknowledgement or follow-up reminder only after the customer approves the exact wording and behavior.
6. Test with synthetic records and provide a short handover guide.

Excluded from the pilot:
- Bulk outreach, purchased contact lists, scraping private data, or unsolicited messaging.
- Autonomous sales negotiation, quoting, or promises to customers.
- Payment processing, financial advice, or access to bank accounts.
- Medical, legal, or other sensitive-data workflows.
- Complex multi-channel CRM migrations or custom mobile apps.

## Initial customer profile

Start with small, non-regulated service businesses that receive enquiries by email or a simple web form and currently track them manually. Do not target healthcare or other sensitive-data sectors in the first pilot.

## Price hypotheses — not validated market rates

- Pilot: EUR 149, one intake channel and one tracker.
- Standard setup after a successful pilot: EUR 299, subject to scope.
- Optional maintenance: EUR 39/month, only if recurring support is requested.

Never represent these figures as a customer's stated budget. Confirm scope, integrations, access, taxes, and payment terms before accepting work.

## Delivery and acceptance

1. Discovery: customer confirms current process, intake channel, owner, and desired response.
2. Written scope: list fields, notification recipient, acknowledgement wording, follow-up timing, exclusions, and price.
3. Build in a test environment with synthetic data.
4. Customer reviews and approves the workflow.
5. Run acceptance tests: new enquiry captured once; required fields present; notification reaches owner; status can be changed; follow-up reminder triggers as agreed; duplicate handling behaves safely.
6. Deploy to customer-owned accounts only after explicit authorization.
7. Handover: short guide, ownership details, rollback steps, and support window.
8. Record payment as revenue only after receipt is confirmed.

## Data protection and security

- Use customer-owned accounts and least-privilege access.
- Do not copy customer personal data into this public repository or Money Agent reports.
- Collect only fields necessary to deliver the agreed workflow.
- Agree retention, access, and deletion arrangements before deployment.
- Do not enable outbound customer messages until the customer approves content, recipients, and trigger conditions.
- Keep a human review step for unusual, duplicate, or high-value enquiries.

## Go/no-go criteria for scaling

Do not expand the offer until at least one paid pilot has been delivered and accepted. Record actual delivery time, defects, customer feedback, support burden, and collected revenue. Reprice only from observed evidence.
